# TimeLapse Pro — Full-Population Compliance Audit Mandate
Date: 2026-10-10. Status: PLANNED, NOT EXECUTED.

## Non-negotiable audit rule
Inventory EVERY normative requirement in EVERY selected applicable edition and amendment. Do not cherry-pick implemented controls. Licensed ISO/IEC text must be obtained lawfully; until then report COVERAGE INCOMPLETE rather than fabricate clause lists or compliance percentages. Import actual Compliance UI catalog and GRC mappings before counting.

## Framework inventory and applicability
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (clauses and Annex A, plus 27002 mapping if licensed); IEC 62443-2-4, 3-3, 4-2, and assess 4-1/3-2/2-1 applicability; GDPR and Danish surveillance law; NIS2 and Danish implementation; CER; EU CRA; EU AI Act; EU Data Act; EU Cybersecurity Act; relevant ePrivacy, product safety and contractual/customer controls where applicable. Check latest legal effective dates and applicability per actor, product, sector, use case and site; do not conflate voluntary standards with binding legislation.

## Each requirement row
framework | edition | requirement ID | exact licensed reference | scope determination | detailed N/A justification | responsible actor | target maturity or SL (only if framework defines it) | implementation maturity (project-defined 0-5, explicitly NOT a standards claim) | code/design/operational evidence | verification method/date | outcome (PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL/NOT TESTED/N/A) | residual risk | owner | remediation | GRC ID.

## Coverage metrics
Population = all identified requirements. Publish totals for applicable, N/A, applicability disputed, not assessed, tested, pass, partial, fail. Report (a) applicability decision coverage, (b) tested share of applicable, (c) pass share of applicable, (d) evidence-backed share. Keep N/A outside pass numerator; show N/A share separately. No fabricated maturity or security level claims. Missing evidence means NOT VERIFIED, never PASS.

## Independence and gates
First audit full population against source and GRC; second auditor challenges N/A and evidence; third reconciles disputes. All deviations and risk acceptances need documented owner and rationale. Audit is not certification or legal opinion. Do not modify authoritative GRC until reviewed.
